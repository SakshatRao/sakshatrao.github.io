Little Brown Pot
---
A lifetime of friendship.
---
I remember the day my mother walked in with a little brown pot in her hand.
“This is for you to plant and grow and take care of,” she made me understand.

Laughing and jumping down the stairs, I hurriedly ran towards our yard,
Dug a hole and carefully placed my plant in it with delicate care and regard.

Every morning before going to school, I would water my growing friend.
Over my childhood years, beyond my height, I gleefully watched it ascend.

And today, it stands as a mighty structure beside my homely remains.
Nobody lives anymore to care for it, nobody to share its joys and pains.

As I touch its bark, which is not much different to my own old wrinkly skin,
I can see the entire life of my friend and I feel my entire life too within.
